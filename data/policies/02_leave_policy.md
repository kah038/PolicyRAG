# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent procedures for requesting, approving, and managing employee leave while ensuring business continuity and compliance with applicable labour legislation.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees of NamTech Solutions Ltd. Temporary employees and interns may receive leave benefits according to their employment agreements.

---

# 3. Annual Leave

Permanent employees are entitled to **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees are encouraged to take annual leave to promote health, wellbeing, and work-life balance.

Annual leave should be requested at least **two weeks in advance** using the company's leave management system.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward for a maximum of **six months**, subject to management approval.

---

# 4. Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work due to illness or injury must notify their immediate supervisor before the start of the working day whenever reasonably possible.

A medical certificate is required for:

- Sick leave exceeding two consecutive working days.
- Frequent recurring absences where requested by Human Resources.

The company may request additional medical information where permitted by law.

---

# 5. Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to maternity leave in accordance with applicable labour legislation.

Employees should notify Human Resources as early as reasonably possible to allow effective workforce planning.

---

# 6. Paternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to **five (5) working days** of paid paternity leave following the birth or legal adoption of a child.

Leave should normally be taken within thirty days of the birth or adoption.

---

# 7. Compassionate Leave

Compassionate leave may be granted following the death or serious illness of an immediate family member.

Normally, employees may receive up to **five working days** of compassionate leave, subject to management approval.

Supporting documentation may be requested where appropriate.

---

# 8. Study Leave

Employees enrolled in approved academic or professional development programmes may apply for study leave.

Approval depends on:

- Relevance of the programme.
- Operational requirements.
- Availability of supporting documentation.

Study leave is granted at the discretion of management.

---

# 9. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to all officially recognized public holidays observed by the company.

Where operational requirements require employees to work on a public holiday, compensation shall be provided in accordance with company policy and applicable labour legislation.

---

# 10. Leave Application Procedure

Employees shall:

1. Submit leave requests through the Human Resources Leave Management System.
2. Obtain supervisor approval before commencing leave.
3. Provide supporting documents where required.
4. Ensure proper handover of responsibilities before extended leave.

---

# 11. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Planning leave responsibly.
- Providing accurate information.
- Complying with approval procedures.

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing leave requests fairly.
- Ensuring operational continuity.
- Maintaining adequate staffing levels.

---

# 12. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy, including unauthorized absence or falsification of leave information, may result in disciplinary action.
