# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Data Privacy Policy is to ensure that NamTech Solutions Ltd collects, processes, stores, and protects personal information responsibly and in compliance with applicable data protection legislation.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees.
- Job applicants.
- Customers.
- Suppliers.
- Contractors.
- Business partners.
- Any individual whose personal information is processed by the company.

---

# 3. Privacy Principles

NamTech Solutions Ltd is committed to:

- Lawful processing of personal information.
- Transparency.
- Data minimization.
- Accuracy.
- Confidentiality.
- Integrity.
- Accountability.

Personal information shall only be collected for legitimate business purposes.

---

# 4. Collection of Personal Data

The company may collect personal information including:

- Full names.
- Contact information.
- National identification details.
- Employment information.
- Banking details where required.
- Emergency contact information.

Only information necessary for business purposes shall be collected.

---

# 5. Use of Personal Data

Personal information shall only be used for:

- Employment administration.
- Payroll processing.
- Customer service.
- Contract management.
- Legal compliance.
- Business operations.

Personal information shall not be used for unauthorized purposes.

---

# 6. Data Storage and Security

Personal information shall be:

- Stored securely.
- Protected using appropriate technical and organizational measures.
- Accessible only to authorized personnel.
- Protected against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Electronic records shall be protected through approved security controls.

---

# 7. Data Sharing

Personal information shall only be shared:

- Where required by law.
- With authorized service providers.
- With the individual's consent where required.
- For legitimate business purposes.

The company shall not sell personal information.

---

# 8. Data Subject Rights

Individuals may request:

- Access to their personal information.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion where legally permitted.
- Restriction of processing.
- Information regarding how their data is used.

Requests shall be handled in accordance with applicable legislation.

---

# 9. Data Retention

Personal information shall only be retained for as long as necessary to:

- Meet legal obligations.
- Fulfil contractual requirements.
- Support legitimate business purposes.

Information shall be securely destroyed when no longer required.

---

# 10. Data Breach Reporting

Any suspected data breach must be reported immediately to:

- Information Security Officer.
- Data Protection Officer.
- Immediate Supervisor.

The company shall investigate all reported data breaches promptly.

---

# 11. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for protecting personal information.

Managers shall ensure compliance within their departments.

The Information Security and Human Resources departments shall oversee implementation of this policy.

---

# 12. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Disciplinary action.
- Suspension of system access.
- Legal action where appropriate.

---

# 13. Document Control

**Policy Owner:** Data Protection Officer
