# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that all employees use NamTech Solutions Ltd information technology resources responsibly, securely, and in a manner that supports business operations.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Interns.
- Temporary staff.
- Third-party users granted access to company systems.

---

# 3. Acceptable Use Principles

Company technology resources shall be used primarily for legitimate business purposes.

Employees are expected to:

- Protect company assets.
- Follow information security requirements.
- Use company systems responsibly.
- Respect applicable laws and regulations.

Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with business operations or violate company policies.

---

# 4. Use of Company Computers

Employees must:

- Lock their computers when leaving their workstations.
- Log off at the end of the working day.
- Protect devices from theft or damage.
- Store business information only in approved company locations.

Computers must not be used by unauthorized persons.

---

# 5. Internet Usage

Internet access is provided to support business activities.

Employees must not:

- Visit malicious or illegal websites.
- Download pirated software.
- Circumvent company security controls.
- Access inappropriate or offensive material.

Business-related research and professional development are encouraged.

---

# 6. Email Usage

Company email shall be used professionally.

Employees must:

- Verify unknown senders.
- Avoid opening suspicious attachments.
- Report phishing attempts immediately.
- Use appropriate language in business communications.

Company email must not be used to distribute confidential information without authorization.

---

# 7. Software Installation

Only software approved by the IT Department may be installed.

Employees must not:

- Install unauthorized software.
- Disable antivirus software.
- Modify security configurations.

Requests for additional software shall be submitted through the IT Service Desk.

---

# 8. Use of Personal Devices

Personal devices used for company work must:

- Be protected by passwords.
- Use updated antivirus software.
- Follow company security requirements.

The company reserves the right to restrict access from non-compliant devices.

---

# 9. Social Media

Employees should behave professionally on social media when representing NamTech Solutions Ltd.

Confidential company information must never be shared on public platforms.

Employees must avoid comments that could damage the company's reputation.

---

# 10. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited:

- Unauthorized system access.
- Sharing passwords.
- Downloading malicious software.
- Illegal file sharing.
- Excessive personal internet usage.
- Harassment or offensive communications.
- Cryptocurrency mining using company equipment.
- Running personal businesses using company resources.

---

# 11. Monitoring

The company reserves the right to monitor:

- Internet usage.
- Email usage.
- System access logs.
- Network activity.

Monitoring shall be conducted in accordance with applicable laws and company policies.

---

# 12. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for using company technology responsibly.

Managers shall ensure policy awareness within their departments.

The IT Department shall maintain secure and reliable systems.

---

# 13. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Disciplinary action.
- Suspension of system access.
- Termination of employment where appropriate.
- Legal action where applicable.

---

# 14. Document Control

**Policy Owner:** Information Technology Manager

**Approved By:** Chief Executive Officer

**Review Cycle:** Every 12 months
