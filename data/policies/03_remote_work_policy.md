# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish guidelines for remote and hybrid working arrangements while ensuring productivity, collaboration, information security, and employee wellbeing.

NamTech Solutions Ltd recognizes that flexible work arrangements can improve operational efficiency, employee satisfaction, and business continuity when managed effectively.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who have been formally approved to work remotely or under a hybrid work arrangement.

Remote work is considered a privilege and not an automatic entitlement.

---

# 3. Eligibility

Eligibility for remote work depends on:

- Job responsibilities.
- Operational requirements.
- Employee performance.
- Information security considerations.
- Managerial approval.

Positions requiring regular physical presence may not qualify for remote work.

---

# 4. Remote Work Schedule

Approved employees may work remotely for up to **three (3) days per week**, subject to departmental requirements.

Managers may require employees to attend the office for:

- Team meetings.
- Client meetings.
- Training sessions.
- Performance reviews.
- Business-critical activities.

Working hours remain:

| Day | Hours |
|------|--------|
| Monday – Friday | 08:00 – 17:00 |

Employees must remain available during official working hours.

---

# 5. Employee Responsibilities

Employees working remotely are responsible for:

- Maintaining productivity and professionalism.
- Protecting confidential information.
- Using approved company systems.
- Ensuring reliable internet connectivity.
- Maintaining a suitable working environment.
- Attending scheduled virtual meetings.
- Reporting technical problems immediately.

---

# 6. Company Responsibilities

NamTech Solutions Ltd will:

- Provide approved software and collaboration tools.
- Offer technical support where reasonably possible.
- Monitor cybersecurity risks.
- Support employee wellbeing.
- Review remote work arrangements periodically.

---

# 7. Information Security Requirements

Employees must:

- Use company-issued devices where possible.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Connect through the corporate Virtual Private Network (VPN).
- Lock computers when unattended.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi unless connected through the approved VPN.
- Report suspected cybersecurity incidents immediately.

Company information must not be stored on unauthorized personal devices.

---

# 8. Equipment and Technology

The company may provide:

- Laptop computer.
- External monitor.
- Keyboard and mouse.
- Collaboration software.
- VPN access.

Employees remain responsible for protecting all company equipment from loss, theft, or damage.

---

# 9. Communication Expectations

Remote employees are expected to:

- Be available during normal working hours.
- Respond promptly to emails and instant messages.
- Attend scheduled virtual meetings.
- Keep supervisors informed of work progress.
- Update task management systems regularly.

---

# 10. Performance Management

Remote employees are evaluated using the same performance standards as office-based employees.

Performance assessments focus on:

- Quality of work.
- Achievement of objectives.
- Communication.
- Collaboration.
- Timely completion of assigned tasks.

---

# 11. Health and Safety

Employees should maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace at home.

The company encourages employees to:

- Take regular screen breaks.
- Maintain proper posture.
- Report work-related injuries occurring during official working hours.
- Follow health and safety guidance issued by the company.

---

# 12. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in suspension or withdrawal of remote work privileges and may lead to disciplinary action where appropriate.
