# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent procedures for reimbursing employees for approved business expenses incurred while performing official duties on behalf of NamTech Solutions Ltd.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Permanent employees.
- Temporary employees.
- Managers.
- Executives.
- Authorized contractors where reimbursement has been approved.

---

# 3. Reimbursement Principles

Business expenses shall only be reimbursed when they:

- Are necessary for legitimate company business.
- Have prior approval where required.
- Are supported by valid receipts or other acceptable documentation.
- Comply with this policy and applicable financial procedures.

Employees are expected to spend company funds responsibly and economically.

---

# 4. Eligible Business Expenses

The following expenses may qualify for reimbursement when properly approved:

- Business travel expenses.
- Accommodation.
- Meals during approved business travel.
- Public transport, taxi, or approved ride-sharing services.
- Mileage for authorized use of a personal vehicle.
- Parking fees.
- Business communication expenses.
- Conference or training registration fees approved by management.

---

# 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The company will not reimburse:

- Personal entertainment expenses.
- Alcoholic beverages unless specifically authorized for official business events.
- Traffic fines or parking penalties.
- Personal shopping.
- Family travel expenses.
- Luxury upgrades beyond approved travel standards.
- Expenses without supporting documentation, unless exceptional approval has been granted.

---

# 6. Submission Requirements

Expense claims must:

- Be submitted within **30 calendar days** after the expense was incurred.
- Include original receipts or approved electronic copies.
- Clearly state the business purpose of each expense.
- Be submitted using the company's approved expense claim process.

Incomplete claims may be returned for correction.

---

# 7. Approval Process

Expense claims require approval from the employee's immediate supervisor.

Claims exceeding approved financial limits may require additional approval from the Finance Manager.

The Finance Department reserves the right to request additional supporting documentation.

---

# 8. Payment of Reimbursements

Approved claims will normally be reimbursed through payroll or electronic bank transfer within **14 business days** after final approval.

Payment timelines may vary where additional verification is required.

---

# 9. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for:

- Submitting accurate claims.
- Providing supporting documentation.
- Complying with this policy.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving legitimate business expenses.

The Finance Department is responsible for processing approved reimbursements.

---

# 10. Policy Compliance

False or fraudulent expense claims may result in:

- Rejection of the claim.
- Disciplinary action.
- Recovery of funds.
- Legal action where appropriate.

---
