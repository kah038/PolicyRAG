# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Code of Conduct is to establish the ethical standards and professional behaviour expected of all employees of NamTech Solutions Ltd. The policy promotes integrity, accountability, fairness, and respect in all business activities.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Permanent employees.
- Temporary employees.
- Managers.
- Executives.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Interns.
- Third-party representatives acting on behalf of the company.

---

# 3. Core Values

All employees are expected to uphold the following values:

- Integrity.
- Professionalism.
- Accountability.
- Respect.
- Fairness.
- Honesty.
- Teamwork.
- Customer focus.
- Continuous improvement.

Employees shall act in a manner that protects the reputation of NamTech Solutions Ltd.

---

# 4. Professional Conduct

Employees shall:

- Perform their duties honestly and diligently.
- Treat colleagues, customers, suppliers, and visitors with courtesy and respect.
- Follow company policies and applicable laws.
- Maintain high professional standards at all times.
- Avoid behaviour that could damage the company's reputation.

---

# 5. Respect and Workplace Behaviour

NamTech Solutions Ltd is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace.

The company does not tolerate:

- Harassment.
- Bullying.
- Discrimination.
- Intimidation.
- Violence.
- Offensive language.
- Sexual harassment.

All employees are expected to contribute to a positive working environment.

---

# 6. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests.

Examples include:

- Awarding contracts to family members.
- Accepting employment with competitors while employed by the company.
- Using confidential information for personal gain.

Potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed immediately to management.

---

# 7. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may only accept gifts or hospitality that are:

- Modest in value.
- Lawful.
- Appropriate to normal business relationships.
- Unlikely to influence business decisions.

Cash gifts or cash equivalents must never be accepted.

Any gift exceeding company limits must be declared to management.

---

# 8. Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential information relating to:

- Customers.
- Employees.
- Financial information.
- Business strategies.
- Intellectual property.
- Suppliers.

Confidential information must not be disclosed without proper authorization.

---

# 9. Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

NamTech Solutions Ltd has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption.

Employees must never:

- Offer bribes.
- Accept bribes.
- Facilitate improper payments.
- Falsify company records.

Any suspected corruption must be reported immediately.

---

# 10. Company Property

Employees must use company property responsibly.

Company assets include:

- Computers.
- Mobile phones.
- Vehicles.
- Equipment.
- Company funds.
- Software.
- Intellectual property.

Misuse or theft of company property may result in disciplinary or legal action.

---

# 11. Reporting Misconduct

Employees are encouraged to report suspected misconduct in good faith.

Reports may be made to:

- Immediate Supervisor.
- Human Resources.
- Ethics Officer.
- Internal Audit.

The company prohibits retaliation against employees who report genuine concerns.

---

# 12. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for complying with this Code of Conduct.

Managers shall lead by example and promote ethical behaviour.

Human Resources shall provide guidance and training regarding this policy.

---

# 13. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Disciplinary action.
- Suspension.
- Termination of employment.
- Legal action where appropriate.

---

# 14. Document Control

**Policy Owner:** Human Resources Director

**Approved By:** Chief Executive Officer

**Review Cycle:** Every 12 months
