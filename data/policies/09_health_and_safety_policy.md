# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to promote a safe, healthy, and secure working environment for all employees, contractors, visitors, and business partners of NamTech Solutions Ltd.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees.
- Contractors.
- Consultants.
- Visitors.
- Temporary workers.
- Any individual present at company premises.

---

# 3. Health and Safety Principles

NamTech Solutions Ltd is committed to:

- Preventing workplace injuries.
- Preventing occupational illnesses.
- Identifying workplace hazards.
- Reducing workplace risks.
- Promoting employee wellbeing.
- Complying with applicable occupational health and safety legislation.

Safety is everyone's responsibility.

---

# 4. Employee Responsibilities

Employees shall:

- Follow all safety procedures.
- Wear required personal protective equipment (PPE).
- Report unsafe conditions immediately.
- Participate in safety training.
- Use equipment safely.
- Cooperate during emergency procedures.

Employees shall not intentionally endanger themselves or others.

---

# 5. Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Maintaining a safe workplace.
- Conducting regular safety inspections.
- Investigating reported incidents.
- Providing appropriate safety equipment.
- Ensuring employees receive safety training.

---

# 6. Workplace Hazards

Employees must report hazards such as:

- Damaged electrical equipment.
- Fire hazards.
- Slippery floors.
- Unsafe machinery.
- Chemical spills.
- Blocked emergency exits.
- Poor lighting.

Hazards should be reported immediately to a supervisor.

---

# 7. Personal Protective Equipment

Where required, employees shall use:

- Safety helmets.
- Safety shoes.
- High-visibility clothing.
- Protective gloves.
- Eye protection.
- Hearing protection.

PPE must be maintained in good condition.

---

# 8. Emergency Procedures

Employees shall familiarize themselves with:

- Emergency exits.
- Fire assembly points.
- Fire extinguisher locations.
- First aid facilities.
- Emergency contact procedures.

Employees must follow instructions during emergencies.

---

# 9. Incident Reporting

All workplace incidents, injuries, near misses, and unsafe conditions must be reported immediately.

Incident reports shall be submitted to:

- Immediate Supervisor.
- Health and Safety Officer.
- Human Resources where applicable.

---

# 10. Training

Employees shall receive health and safety training:

- During induction.
- When job responsibilities change.
- Following significant workplace changes.
- At regular refresher intervals.

---

# 11. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action and other corrective measures where appropriate.

---

# 12. Document Control
