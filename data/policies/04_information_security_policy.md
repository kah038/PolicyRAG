# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to protect NamTech Solutions Ltd information assets against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, theft, and destruction while ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company information.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All permanent and temporary employees.
- Contractors and consultants.
- Interns.
- Third-party service providers with access to company systems.
- All company-owned information assets.

---

# 3. Information Security Principles

All employees must:

- Protect confidential information.
- Use company information only for authorized business purposes.
- Follow approved security procedures.
- Report security incidents immediately.
- Comply with applicable data protection legislation.

---

# 4. User Access Control

Access to company systems shall be granted according to the principle of least privilege.

Employees shall only have access to information necessary to perform their assigned duties.

User accounts:

- Must not be shared.
- Shall be individually assigned.
- Shall be disabled immediately upon employee termination.

---

# 5. Password Requirements

Passwords must:

- Contain at least 12 characters.
- Include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Not contain personal information.
- Be changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

Passwords must never be shared with another person.

---

# 6. Acceptable Handling of Information

Confidential information shall:

- Be stored only in approved company systems.
- Not be copied onto personal devices without authorization.
- Be securely destroyed when no longer required.

Printed confidential documents must not be left unattended.

---

# 7. Email and Internet Security

Employees must:

- Verify unknown email senders before opening attachments.
- Never click suspicious links.
- Report phishing emails immediately.
- Use company email only for legitimate business activities.

Downloading unauthorized software is prohibited.

---

# 8. Mobile Device Security

Company laptops and mobile devices must:

- Be protected using passwords or biometric authentication.
- Have automatic screen locking enabled.
- Use approved antivirus software.
- Be reported immediately if lost or stolen.

---

# 9. Remote Access

Employees working remotely must:

- Use the approved company VPN.
- Connect only through secure internet connections.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi without VPN protection.
- Ensure confidential information cannot be viewed by unauthorized persons.

---

# 10. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported immediately to:

- IT Service Desk
- Information Security Officer
- Immediate Supervisor

Examples include:

- Lost laptop.
- Malware infection.
- Unauthorized system access.
- Suspected phishing attacks.
- Data leakage.

---

# 11. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for protecting company information.

Managers are responsible for ensuring compliance within their departments.

The IT Department is responsible for implementing technical security controls.

---

# 12. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Disciplinary action.
- Suspension of system access.
- Legal action where applicable.
