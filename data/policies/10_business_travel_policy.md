# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent procedures for planning, approving, and managing official business travel undertaken on behalf of NamTech Solutions Ltd while ensuring employee safety, responsible expenditure, and operational efficiency.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Permanent employees.
- Temporary employees.
- Managers.
- Executives.
- Authorized contractors travelling on official company business.

---

# 3. Business Travel Principles

Business travel shall:

- Support legitimate business objectives.
- Be cost-effective.
- Be properly authorized before travel.
- Prioritize employee safety and wellbeing.
- Comply with company financial policies.

Virtual meetings should be considered where practical before approving travel.

---

# 4. Travel Authorization

All business travel must receive prior approval from the employee's immediate supervisor.

International travel may require additional approval from executive management.

Employees shall not commit the company to travel expenses without authorization.

---

# 5. Travel Arrangements

Travel bookings should be made through approved company procedures whenever possible.

Employees are encouraged to:

- Book travel early.
- Select economical travel options.
- Follow approved travel itineraries.

---

# 6. Accommodation

Reasonable accommodation expenses will be covered for approved business travel.

Accommodation should:

- Be safe.
- Be conveniently located.
- Represent value for money.

Luxury accommodation requires prior management approval.

---

# 7. Meals and Daily Allowances

Employees may receive meal allowances or reimbursement for reasonable meal expenses during approved business travel in accordance with company financial procedures.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless specifically approved for official business events.

---

# 8. Transportation

Approved transportation may include:

- Air travel.
- Rail transport.
- Rental vehicles.
- Public transport.
- Taxis or approved ride-sharing services.
- Authorized use of personal vehicles.

Employees must comply with all traffic laws while travelling.

---

# 9. Travel Expenses

Employees must retain receipts for reimbursable expenses.

Expense claims shall be submitted in accordance with the Expense Reimbursement Policy within **30 calendar days** after completion of travel.

---

# 10. Health and Safety During Travel

Employees travelling on company business should:

- Follow local laws.
- Protect company property.
- Safeguard confidential information.
- Report emergencies immediately.
- Maintain regular communication where appropriate.

---

# 11. International Travel

Employees travelling internationally shall:

- Ensure passports and visas are valid.
- Obtain any required travel insurance.
- Comply with destination country regulations.
- Follow company security guidance for international travel.

---

# 12. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for complying with this policy and managing company travel responsibly.

Managers are responsible for approving necessary travel and ensuring compliance.

The Finance Department shall process approved travel-related reimbursements.

---

# 13. Policy Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in rejection of travel claims, disciplinary action, or other corrective measures where appropriate.

---

# 14. Document Control
